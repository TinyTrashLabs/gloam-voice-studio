## Description (4000 max)

Gloam Voice Studio clones voices and turns text into natural speech, entirely on your Mac. Record or import a short clip, then make single lines or whole scripts in that voice, with moods, pacing and delivery tags. Or chat with a voice: type to it and hear it reply in character, with its own persona and greeting.

Everything runs on Apple Silicon, on the GPU with MLX or on the Neural Engine. No audio is uploaded, no account is needed, and no data is collected.

FEATURES
- Clone a voice from a short recording, a clip from another app, or a file
- Sixteen voice engines, including Qwen3, Chatterbox, Fish S2-Pro, Breeze, Kokoro, LuxTTS and Dia 2, each downloaded inside the app when you want it
- Qwen3 on the Neural Engine: speech starts almost at once and runs beside a local chat model
- Five moods, from flat to hype, and language takes such as Spanish
- Describe a voice in a sentence and audition the voices it makes
- Single-line and full-script modes, and Dialogue for two voices in one take
- Chat with a voice, with streaming replies you hear as they generate
- Voice packs (.gvoice) that carry a voice's takes, persona and avatar, to share or back up
- On-device speech-to-text for transcription and dictation
- Export audio for use anywhere
- Optional local API and MCP server for other apps on your Mac

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Gloam Voice Studio processes everything on this Mac. There's no account, no analytics, and no data sent to us.

Voice cloning is a powerful capability. Only clone voices you have the right to use: your own, or a speaker who has given you permission.

Requires Apple Silicon.
